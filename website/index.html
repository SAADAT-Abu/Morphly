A Features

Everything a figure for a paper needs.

Built for the figures scientists actually make: labelled schematics, multi-panel layouts and graphical abstracts, exported the way journals want them.

Illustration libraries
2,531

Vetted scientific drawings, searchable offline.

NIH BioArt ships inside the app. Add more from the built-in Art Store whenever you want them, each checked on download and labelled with its licence.

Recolour

Change one colour, every part follows.

Morphly reads the palette out of a vector drawing. Try it:

Connectors that stay attached

Straight, curved or elbow lines glue to shapes and illustrations and follow them when they move.

Panel layouts, lettered

Split a page into panels with automatic A, B, C labels and even spacing, the way journal figures are laid out.

True vector export

SVG and PDF that stay sharp at any size, plus PNG and JPEG at 1×, 2× or 4×. Citations can go in the footer.

Your plots, still editable

SVGs from R (ggplot2, svglite) and Python (matplotlib) arrive as recolourable artwork and stay vector.

Autosave and crash recovery

Name a figure and it saves as you work. If something goes wrong, your unsaved changes are offered back.

Edit inside a drawing

Double-click an illustration to recolour, move or hide one piece of it, such as a single organelle.

C Licences made clear

Know what you owe before you submit.

Every illustration carries a badge in the library. Morphly writes the credits for you and warns you before exporting anything with strings attached.

PD

Public domain

Use it anywhere, for anything. Credit is appreciated but not required. Most of NIH BioArt is here.

BY

Attribution

Credit the artist. Tick "Append asset citations" on export and the correctly formatted credits are written into your figure.

SA

Share-alike

Credit required, and your figure may have to carry the same open licence. Fine for open access, worth a thought otherwise.

D Roadmap

Built in small, steady releases.

  1. Editor, libraries and Art Packs

    0.1 to 0.3Released

    The canvas editor, vector recolouring, several figures per file, the Art Store, and builds for Windows, macOS and Linux.

  2. Diagrams and a safety net

    0.4Released

    Glue points, curved and elbow connectors, smart guides, editing parts of a drawing, SVG import, panel layouts, autosave and crash recovery.

  3. Data, graphs and a simpler layout

    0.5In progress

    GraphPad-style datasets and graphs (bar charts with points and error bars, box plots, scatter and XY plots), common statistics checked against R, and a layout that is easier to pick up.

  4. Figures from AI assistants

    0.6Planned

    An MCP server, so assistants such as Claude Code and Codex can build figures on request. The result is an ordinary .morphly file you keep editing by hand, and it all runs locally.
